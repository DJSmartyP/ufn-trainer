THE EXPERIENCE

More than a space battle.

Join a crew of usually 4–6 players, each online from their own device. In EmptyEpsilon, the starship game software, you operate one station while your team talks over Discord and makes decisions together.

UFN means United Federated Navy, the setting for our missions. SXP (Shared XP) is the Discord community where you find games and meet your team.

PICTURE YOUR FIRST MISSION

What do you actually do?

You operate one part of the ship, talk with your crewmates and decide together what to do next. Here is an illustrative moment:

  1. A ship calls for help, but something in its story does not add up.
  2. Your crew shares what they can see, asks questions and weighs the risks.
  3. You choose a course of action. The live characters respond, and the story moves on.

You only need to learn your own station, and we train you before play begins.

Come as you are.Join on your own or with friends. Roleplay is optional, and your Flight Controller will teach you your station.

A few things you might be wondering

01

What the game is like

I’m completely new. Will I know what to do?

Yes! We start with a briefing and training on your station before the mission begins. You can ask questions as you go, and your Flight Controller will guide the crew.

What do we actually do during a mission?

You run one part of a starship with your crew, talk with characters performed live, follow clues and decide what to do together. Every mission has its own surprises.

How long should I set aside?

About 1½–2 hours. That includes the briefing, station training, the mission itself and a team debrief afterwards.

Is it all online?

Yes. Each player joins from their own device over the internet. You use Discord for voice and EmptyEpsilon—or our browser version—for the game.

Do I have to roleplay?

Only if you want to. Some people play a character; others keep things straightforward. Either way is welcome, as long as everyone treats the rest of the crew well.

Are the missions connected?

You can enjoy a standalone mission, or request our connected six-story campaign, which private groups often play together. You don’t need to commit to a campaign to try your first game.

02

Joining a mission

Can I join alone or bring friends?

Both! You can take an open slot and join a crew of usually 4–6 players. If you have a group, ask Smarty in SXP Discord about arranging slots together.

Can I choose my station?

You can pick a first and second choice in Terminal, or say you have no preference. Terminal tries to match you, and your crew can still discuss or change stations right up to the start.

Where do I find and book a game?

Open slots are in Terminal. You can also see upcoming missions in #ufn-deployments on SXP Discord. Once you book, Terminal keeps your mission details.

What if there are no open slots?

Keep an eye on #ufn-deployments, or ask in SXP Discord about a future mission. Already have a group? Smarty can help you arrange slots.

Can I see what missions are available?

Yes. Have a look through the missions on our Training Portal to get a feel for the stories. When you’re ready to play, check Terminal for open slots.

Does it cost anything?

Not at the moment. Joining SXP, using Terminal and playing UFN Missions are all free.

What if I need to cancel?

That happens. Cancel in Terminal or let us know in #ufn-deployments. As much notice as you can give helps us offer the place to someone else.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST GAME

Ready for your first mission.

Bring a reliable internet connection, a Discord account and a working microphone. Headphones or a headset are recommended.

How will you play?

Choose your device or browser to see the setup you need.

Select an option above. Your instructions will appear here.

Windows

Download EmptyEpsilon from the official website or Steam. Check that you have EE-2024.12.08.

Android

Download the matching EE-2024.12.08 APK from the EmptyEpsilon website.

Mac

Use browser access from the top of your Terminal page once the game is live. You can also use BlueStacks with the matching Android APK, or run Windows through Parallels or similar software.

Play in your browser

Browser play works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. The access link appears at the top of your Terminal page once the game is live. Ask your Flight Controller for help when you join the voice channel.

For installed play, match the host version: EmptyEpsilon EE-2024.12.08. Check that the game opens before your session.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST GAME

Your quick checklist

These are the practical bits to have ready. Your Flight Controller will guide you through the game itself.

  • Book in Terminal. Check your mission details and station preferences there.
  • Prepare your device. If you are using the installed game, set up EmptyEpsilon EE-2024.12.08 beforehand and check that it opens. Browser access appears at the top of your Terminal page once your game is live.
  • Get voice ready. Sign in to SXP Discord and check that your microphone works; a headset is recommended.
  • Join a little early. Check your details in Terminal, then look in SXP Discord for the voice channel named after your mission at the start time. Your Flight Controller will guide you from there.
AT GAME TIME

Where do I go?

Check your details in Terminal and join a little early. The voice channel named after your mission usually appears in SXP Discord at your game’s start time. Join it and your Flight Controller will guide you from there.

Open Terminal →Open SXP Discord →

What happens during the session?

Set aside around 1½–2 hours, including training and the debrief.

  1. 01

    Briefing

    Meet the team and find out what your mission is about.

  2. 02

    Station training

    Learn your controls, try things out and ask questions before the mission starts.

  3. 03

    The mission

    Work with your crew as new information, characters and challenges enter the story.

  4. 04

    Debrief

    Talk through the experience together. Medals or promotions may also be awarded.

03

Getting ready to play

What do I need to have ready?

A reliable internet connection, a Discord account and a working microphone. Headphones or a headset help too. If you’re using the installed game, set it up before your session and join a little early.

Do I have to install EmptyEpsilon?

If you want to use the game app, install the matching EE-2024.12.08 version beforehand. You can also play in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari; the browser link appears at the top of Terminal when your game is live.

Can I play on a Mac or Android device?

Yes. Android players can get the matching APK from the EmptyEpsilon website. On Mac, the easiest option is browser play through Terminal when your game is live. BlueStacks with the APK or Windows through Parallels are other options.

Where do I go when my game starts?

Check your mission details in Terminal and join a little early. The voice channel named after your mission usually appears in SXP Discord at the start time. Join it and your Flight Controller will take it from there.

Can you make adjustments if I need them?

We’ll do what we can. Message Smarty in SXP Discord before your game, or talk to your Flight Controller on the day. We can often use less text or voice, or reduce the voice effects on characters. Tell us what would help.
